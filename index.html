How Loan Poacher works

  1. Enter how much you need to borrow and the monthly payment you can afford.
  2. Add optional details (credit score, citizenship, state, cosigner) for more personal results. Results update as you type.
  3. Scroll to the recommendations: each lender is rated out of 10, and "View loan" opens its website.

The color circles above only change how the app looks.
